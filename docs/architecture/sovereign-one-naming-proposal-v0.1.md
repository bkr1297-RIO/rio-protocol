## 5. Constitutional Boundary

Sovereignty here is reciprocal, not dominating.

The system protects each human’s authority over their own agency, consent, identity, and consequence. It does not allow one human’s authority to erase another human’s authority. It does not allow a model, institution, proxy, memory layer, receipt, pattern, convergence, or capability to become sovereign over the human.

Keeper lines:

- The human is the sovereign one.
- The system serves sovereignty; it does not possess it.
- Sovereignty is reciprocal: no sovereign one may take sovereignty from another.
- ONE is the environment. The human is the sovereign one.
- Human authority remains final, but bounded by the equal sovereignty of others.
