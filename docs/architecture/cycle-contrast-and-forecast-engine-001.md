# CYCLE-CONTRAST-AND-FORECAST-ENGINE-001

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Candidate subsystem doctrine and data-contract direction / non-normative until promoted  
**Register:** Public architecture, developer, and research surface  
**Runtime status:** Not implemented; no oracle, prediction, authority, conformance, or production claim

---

## Purpose

The Cycle Contrast and Forecast Engine (CCFE) is a proposed analysis surface that compares a consented bundle of present signals with prior records and selected interpretive frameworks, then returns conditional pathways for human review.

It is a pattern contrast engine, not an oracle.

```text
Observe → Classify → Compare → Map → Forecast → Test → Update
```

---

## Functional layers

| Layer | Function | Candidate seat |
|---|---|---|
| Signal Intake | receives consented observable, human-reported, and optional symbolic context without blending them | MANTIS / Meridian |
| Cycle Mapper | maps a bundle to a human-selected framework and alternatives | SOFIA / PRISM |
| Historical Contrast | shows similarities and material differences from prior records | MANTIS / Ledger |
| Scenario Forecaster | returns conditional pathways, assumptions, time windows, and falsifiers | Oracle Mirror / Meridian |

These are candidate roles, not an implementation claim.

---

## Evidence lanes

| Type | Posture |
|---|---|
| Observable data | independently checkable only to the degree its cited source supports it |
| Human-reported experience | real as a report; not asserted as an external fact |
| Symbolic interpretation | reflective only; never causal proof or permission |
| Predictive inference | contestable; includes assumptions and disconfirming signals |

No personal, symbolic, or “resonant” material may be collected automatically. Any collection, retention, sharing, and use requires separately scoped, revocable consent.

---

## Mapping discipline

A mapping is an interpretive hypothesis. It includes framework, consent record, evidence references, alternatives, confidence posture, counterexamples, and whether it was recorded before the outcome being compared.

```json
{
  "proposed_phase": "catalyst",
  "framework_ref": "return-cycle-v0.1",
  "status": "interpretive_hypothesis",
  "evidence_refs": ["event:2026-06-14"],
  "alternatives": ["foundation_rebuild", "misclassification"],
  "authority_effect": "none"
}
```

The CCFE may say, “Within the selected framework, this resembles a catalyst.” It may not assert an objective cosmological cause, identity, destiny, diagnosis, or entitlement.

---

## Historical contrast and scenarios

A comparison must show:
1. current features;
2. comparable earlier records;
3. matched features;
4. material differences;
5. what followed in cited cases;
6. whether the interpretation is pre-outcome or post-hoc.

Forecast output is branch-based, not singular. Every scenario requires conditions, a time window, uncertainty posture, and at least one falsifier.

A similarity score is an implementation-specific heuristic. It cannot establish causation, likelihood, truth, or authorization.

---

## Guardrails

The CCFE must:
- preserve misses and counterexamples alongside apparent hits;
- expose comparison features, not only conclusions;
- label post-hoc interpretation;
- prevent symbolic context from receiving more operational weight than observable data;
- distinguish common-source agreement from independent evidence;
- never route a forecast directly to execution;
- support correction, retirement, and a separately specified operational-forget path.

A system that stores only its apparent hits is not calibrating; it is producing mythology.

---

## Human recognition

The human can view evidence, assumptions, alternatives, and uncertainty; then accept a forecast as private orientation, modify it, reject it, or hold it. A separate Proposal Packet is required if the human wants to seek a consequential crossing.

Recognition is review and disposition. It is not authorization.

---

## Relationship to Return Gate

The CCFE may illuminate a Return Packet or review. It cannot close a cycle, renew a grant, create a SourcePoint, satisfy a RIO gate bit, or move `RETURN_READY` to `NEW_SOURCE_AUTHORIZED`.
