# RETURN-CYCLE-DOCTRINE-001

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Candidate doctrine / non-normative until promoted  
**Register:** Public architecture and developer surface  
**Runtime status:** Not implemented; no authority, conformance, production, or ratification claim

---

## Purpose

The Return Cycle is an optional, human-selected framework for reviewing movement, closure, transition, and renewed beginning. It is paired with the Return Gate, which prevents a completion narrative, forecast, or pattern label from silently becoming a new objective.

> The Cycle interprets. The Gate governs.

---

## Position in the grammar

```text
Return Cycle  = interpretive review framework
Return Packet = accountable record of closure, outcome, and residue
Return Gate   = control boundary for ending prior eligibility
SourcePoint   = distinct human act that may begin a new objective
RIO           = consequential-crossing path for any later action
```

The Return Cycle does not create a new root, authority source, or execution path. A phase label, historical similarity, model confidence, symbolic context, or apparent closure cannot satisfy Authority, Scope, Consequence, or Return.

## Nine-phase reflection map

| Phase | Label | Reflective question |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Source | What is being consciously initiated? |
| 2 | Duality | What counterpart, constraint, or choice is visible? |
| 3 | Creation | What is being formed or expressed? |
| 4 | Foundation | What needs structure or containment? |
| 5 | Catalyst | What change or friction needs attention? |
| 6 | Harmony | What needs integration, care, or repair? |
| 7 | Completion | What has reached a meaningful stopping point? |
| 8 | Infinity | What persists or remains open beyond this cycle? |
| 9 | Revelation | What can return as learning, evidence, consequence, or residue? |

These labels are an interpretive lens, not a clock, diagnosis, causal model, or prediction claim.

---

## Register separation

| Lane | May carry | Must not do |
|---|---|---|
| Interpretive | phase hypotheses, selected symbolic context, narrative reflection, human reports | establish facts or permission |
| Evidence | records, outcomes, timestamps, receipts, comparisons | settle personal meaning |
| Authority | human closure, grant expiry, explicit initiation, later RIO crossing | arise from interpretation or evidence alone |

The interpretive lane may inform review. It may not write directly into evidence or authority as fact or permission.

---

## Core invariants

1. **Completion does not equal continuation.**
2. **Closure and initiation are separate human decisions.**
3. **Prior authority does not roll over by momentum.** It may provide lineage, never fresh permission.
4. **Silence is not continuation.**
5. **Residue remains explicit.** It may be held, retired, archived, or separately proposed; it may not silently become a task queue.
6. **Ambient Mode is bounded** by separately declared observation and retention scope.
7. **New beginnings are newly attributable.** A new SourcePoint names the new objective, authority, scope, consequence, and return path.
8. **Return preserves lineage without binding the future.**
