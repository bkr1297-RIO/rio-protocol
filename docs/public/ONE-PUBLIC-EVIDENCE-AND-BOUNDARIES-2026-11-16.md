## The claim taxonomy

- **Founded** means a human-originated, attributable research or architectural object has been named and constituted.
- **Specified** means a particular formal or operational contract is recorded.
- **Implemented** means exact source performs some declared behavior in a defined environment.
- **Tested** means a specific executed suite produced an attributable result.
- **Observed** means an actual event was witnessed within declared coverage and uncertainty.
- **Reproduced** means a separate invocation reconstructs the stated result under known conditions.
- **Adopted/released** means an authorized human or institution has made its own declaration. Neither a merge nor publicity creates a scientific truth claim.

Claims may hold at several of these standings simultaneously but cannot pass from one to another automatically.

## How to interpret evidence

A valid signature may establish a relationship between bytes and a key, not the actual legitimacy of the signer. A receipt may establish an internally consistent account, not external truth. A repository may contain code that does not currently run at its public URL. A theorem may be correct under axioms narrower than the proposed cross-domain law. A model simulation may be reproducible because a rule was deliberately programmed.

**Do not collapse capability into authority, prediction into permission, observation into evidence, or Return into successor installation.**

A working public protocol is distinct from an authentic, source-qualified multi-user ONE experience. The private Founder environment requires additional authorization, security, custody and whole-cycle checks before it can be described as commissioned.

## Publication and rights

- The repository's actual licensing controls; **public visibility does not grant reuse rights**. Current RIO Protocol is All Rights Reserved.
- Private Founder HOME records, personal meanings, unpublished source, uploaded source bundles, keys and third-party information are not included in this public release packet.
- No invitation to submit sensitive data to an experimental public app is made.
- The public research introduction is descriptive and non-operative. It does not amend RIO's project normative standards.

## The operational checksum

**What happened? Whose was it? Under what standing? What may rightfully follow?**

A public claim should always preserve all four answers.
