### Central proposition

**The Law of Generative Relational Transformation** is a research formulation about how historically constituted organizations alter the possibilities accessible to later transformations, retaining differentiated continuity with their causes.

**Governing principle:** Constitutive Emergence Through Differentiated Continuity.

Six complementary mathematical responsibilities: **Relation · Passage · Difference · Continuity · Emergence · Return**. These describe obligations and observables, not a compulsory six-stage execution sequence.

A source-relative transformation grammar:

$$
\boxed{R_t\xrightarrow{P_t}(R_{t+1},\Delta_t,\mathcal A_{t+1})}
$$

Here \(R_t\) is a relational configuration, \(P_t\) an actual passage, \(\Delta_t\) the material difference including loss and unresolved remainder, and \(\mathcal A_{t+1}\) the accessible repertoire relative to a declared question, boundary and horizon.

The fixed-law / varying-accessibility distinction is:

$$
\mathcal L_{t+1}=\mathcal L_t
\quad\text{while possibly}\quad
\mathcal A(R_{t+1})\neq\mathcal A(R_t).
$$

It does **not** assert that every novelty is a new fundamental physical possibility or that every system grows monotonically.

### Three generative mathematical objects

| Object | Inquiry | Conceptual status |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Constitutive Reachability** | What transformations or generators are accessible from this configuration given an effective regime and declared horizon? | Defined relative to model, boundary, resources and comparison class |
| **Recursive Constitutive Emergence** | Can a generated capability participate as a mechanism of generating a further capability? | Mechanism of historical generativity, not a standalone universally proved physical law |
| **Constitutive Lineage** | Through what historically reconstructable passages did those generators arise, persist, mutate, branch, or disappear? | A source-attributed genealogy; an internal provenance record is not automatically external event proof |

The distinction between newly formed state, accessible capability and recursively reused generative capability is material. Two systems with equal present selected-task output can have different reachable future generator sets.

A profile-relative accessible repertoire can be expressed as:

$$
\mathcal A_t^{K,h}=\operatorname{Reach}_{\mathcal L,K}^{h}(S_t,O_t,Q_t,E_t)
$$

where \(K\) specifies the capability question and \(h\) the time/resource horizon. Claimed novelty must identify which relevant conditions are held comparable.

An abstract functional-lineage carrier is:

$$
\Lambda_t=(D_t,P_t^{\mathrm{prov}})
$$

with \(D_t\) the constituted generator organization under an effective regime and \(P_t^{\mathrm{prov}}\) its recorded causal provenance. **Do not confuse** this paper's provenance coordinate with the earlier \(P_t^{\mathrm{passage}}\) actual passage, or a regime \(R_t^{\mathrm{regime}}\) with the wider relational configuration \(R_t^{\mathrm{relation}}\).

### Continuity and the arrow

Equal endpoints do not establish equivalent history under every material question \(K\):

$$
\mathrm{End}(P)=\mathrm{End}(Q)\ \not\Rightarrow\ P\equiv_K Q.
$$

A particular transformed result may remain causally descended while losing some former capabilities. Historical continuity, numerical identity, operating capacity and legitimate normative standing are separate claims.

For ONE's constitutional specialization, a candidate-producing transformation, succession judgment and actual installation have different conditions. The formal Passage Keel record distinguishes these and does not regard computational success as a grant of human authority.

### Actual evidence and claim ceilings

The originating research contains a historically ratified Passage Keel and reported scoped Lean mathematical verification (Formal Core F1). Those results apply only to their formalized models, explicit assumptions and source. They do not demonstrate a universal physics theorem or production enforcement of every ONE runtime component.

The originating computational research also contains two **different** bounded source specimens:

- **Emergence Lab 001:** a JavaScript toy ecosystem with fixed, adaptive and recursive rules. A separate source reproduction recovered the recorded 24-seed × 3-condition experiment, with increased model-defined capability depth for recursively reusable constructs. The distinction among treatments is part of the authored simulation rules; that is not independent proof that the generative rules evolve naturally.
- **Constitutive Lineage v0.1:** a finite Python generator-catalog reference with four locally passing checks for divergent future reachability, generator-construction paths, loss/regime constraints and internally consistent hash-linked history. The demo approval flag is not authenticated human authority.

These examples establish specific, bounded **program behavior**. Reproducing a programmed effect is useful evidence but cannot substitute for independent empirical comparison or a mathematical proof of all cross-domain instances.

### Scientific and engineering outlook

The proposed unifying object is not one new physical force. It is **the transformation of the conditions through which further transformation becomes accessible**, examined with material historical lineage and explicit loss. In the computational specialization, beneficial capability expansion must remain answerable and must not independently enlarge authority.

**No claim here** implies consciousness, scientific priority over existing evolutionary accounts, the existence of a new fundamental law of physics, or whole-machine independent certification.
