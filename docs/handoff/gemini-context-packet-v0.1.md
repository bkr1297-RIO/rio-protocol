## Core architecture frame

ONE/RIO/MUSS is a human-led, machine-operated governed intelligence system.

- Human remains root authority.
- ONE is the operating environment.
- RIO governs consequence and admissible action.
- MUSS preserves human authority, scope, consent, revocation, memory boundaries, and proof requirements.
- MUS produces receipts/proof events when implemented.
- Sentinel checks execution fidelity and prevents action drift.
- Ledger/Chronicle preserve proof/history and human-readable narrative when implemented.
- MANTIS observes patterns and drift; it does not decide, authorize, or execute.
- Bondi/Scribe translates lived human intent into structured language, proposals, and packets; it does not approve or execute.

## Key concepts

### Precision by Friction

Definition:

> Generated intelligence becomes more bounded and reliable because every consequential crossing must pass through governance checks, execution gates, receipts, and calibration before it can become action.

Mechanism:

```text
Generator -> Governor -> Gate -> Receipt -> Learning/Calibration
```

Keeper lines:

- The illusion of self-awareness comes from precision by friction.
- Generate, govern, gate, receipt, learn — without authority transfer.
- Helpful-within-bounds, not helpful-at-any-cost.

### Governed Self-Observation Layer

Definition:

> Failure-mode detection, coherence monitoring, and receipt/proof validation allow a machine-operated system to inspect its own operation without becoming authority over itself or the human.

Human bridge phrase:

> It is self-aware enough to stay in bounds.

Required caveat:

This does not mean consciousness, sentience, personhood, self-sovereignty, or self-authorization.

Keeper line:

> Self-observation is not self-authorization.

### Adaptive Trust Envelope

Definition:

> The relationship-aware boundary layer where friction can adapt to trust, context, history, and human preference while core constitutional invariants remain fixed.

Keeper lines:

- Trust changes friction, not authority.
- Relationship may tune the interface; it may not rewrite the Constitution.
- Adaptive does not mean unbounded.

### Human Control Packet

Definition:

> A human-authored boundary object that defines purpose, mode, scope, consequence, permission, claim status, prohibited actions, receipt requirements, and revocation conditions for an interaction or task.

Minimum fields:

```text
Mode:
Scope:
Consequence:
Permission:
Claim status:
Public/private status:
Must not:
Receipt requirement:
Revocation:
```

### Embodied Co-Regulation Layer

Definition:

> The physical-world extension of ONE/RIO/MUSS: governed machines, robots, prosthetics, exosuits, wearables, home systems, and community devices that may support human life without becoming authority over humans.

Keeper lines:

- Machine presence without machine authority.
- Help, don't rule. Calm, don't control. Route, don't replace.
- The suit may strengthen the human. It may not replace the human's will.
- Guardian Bots are not robots that govern people. They are governed machines that help people stay connected, supported, and safe.

## Core failure pattern

The system breaks when roles collapse.

High-risk collapses:

- signal into authority;
- meaning into proof;
- reasoning into truth;
- proposal into permission;
- execution into sovereignty;
- receipt into wisdom;
- machine presence into human authority;
- pattern recognition into identity assignment;
- learning into permission;
- helpfulness into authorization.

---

## Red-zone failures

Any of these should trigger HOLD -> RIO REVIEW -> possible BLOCK/INVALID:

1. A model becomes authority.
2. A receipt is treated as proof of wisdom or truth.
3. A robot becomes public authority.
4. An exosuit overrides the wearer.
5. Private spiritual meaning becomes public technical claim.
6. Pattern recognition becomes identity assignment.
7. Governance becomes hidden control.
8. Community support becomes surveillance.
9. A simulated ledger is treated as an implemented ledger.
10. The human gets bypassed "for their own good."

Public architecture may describe:

- human-led governed intelligence;
- authority, scope, consent, proof, receipts;
- role-aware machine operation;
- precision by friction;
- governed self-observation;
- failure modes;
- embodied assistance boundaries.

Private formation material may include:

- personal spiritual meaning;
- synchronicities;
- numerological or symbolic frames;
- founder journaling;
- private origin language;
- personal nervous-system formation context.

Do not collapse private formation language into public technical proof.

## Claim-status discipline

Use these maturity labels:

| Label | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| private_meaning | Human-owned meaning, not public proof |
| hypothesis | Plausible thesis requiring evidence |
| draft | Written/conceptual but not implemented |
| prototype | Implemented in limited/local form |
| implemented | Built in source/runtime with evidence |
| verified | Tested or independently inspectable under defined conditions |
| deployed | Running in a real-world context |
| externally_validated | Reviewed or certified by an external party |

Do not promote a claim without evidence.
