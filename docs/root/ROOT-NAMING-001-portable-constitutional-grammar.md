**Purpose:** Provide a compact naming layer for orienting new contributors, model-review threads, and protocol-adjacent documentation without importing private symbolic material as public proof.

## 8. Register Separation Rule

Private symbolic, personal, spiritual, or archetypal material may inspire naming, metaphor, or formation work, but it does not function as public proof, technical authorization, conformance evidence, enterprise claim, or execution permission.

Repository-safe material must be expressed as:

1. protocol terms,
2. schemas,
3. validators,
4. fixtures,
5. receipts,
6. conformance levels,
7. falsifiable claims,
8. implementation-neutral doctrine.
